# Tonalli user guide

A step-by-step tour of Tonalli on **Android** and **Windows**, with screenshots. The iPhone app uses
the same screens. Each page below covers one screen or task.

Tonalli is free and works offline. After you download the content you want, you can read, search and
use maps with no connection.

Spanish speakers: see the [quick guide in Spanish](LEEME.md).

## First steps

1. Open Tonalli and go to **Library**.
2. Press **Get content**, pick a book, map or file collection, and download it. You can pause, cancel
   and resume a download.
3. When it finishes, it appears in **Library**. Open it to read and search.
4. Everything stays on your device. Notes are encrypted with a passphrase you choose.

## Notes about the screenshots

- Screenshots were taken on a Pixel 6 Pro (Android) and on Windows 11, using test content only.
- Anything personal (addresses, pairing codes, names, messages, notes) is left out of the pictures.
- Sharing is off until you turn it on, and works only on your local network.
